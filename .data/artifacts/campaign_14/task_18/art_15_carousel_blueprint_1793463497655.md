# Carousel Blueprint: Day 4: Bean-to-Cup Craftsmanship Carousel

**Theme**: Bean-to-Cup Craftsmanship & Educational Carousel

**Target Audience**: Specialty coffee enthusiasts and home brewers seeking artisanal craft education.

## Slides Breakdown (5 slides)

### Slide 1: 1. Single-Origin Transparency
- **Copy**: Sourcing from single-origin farms means tasting the true terroir of the coffee cherry. From Ethiopian floral notes to Colombian caramel sweetness.
- **Visual Concept**: Green coffee beans in burlap sack with origin map overlay.

### Slide 2: 2. Artisanal Roasting Craft
- **Copy**: Our master roasters dial in every batch by ear, smell, and color to unlock peak flavor profiles without bitterness.
- **Visual Concept**: Roaster drum spinning with glowing roast light and smoke rising.

### Slide 3: 3. The Grind Rule
- **Copy**: Grind right before brewing. The golden window for peak aromatics is within 15 minutes of grinding your beans.
- **Visual Concept**: Burr grinder crushing specialty beans into fluffy grounds.

### Slide 4: 4. Precise Water Temp
- **Copy**: Water temperature between 200°F and 205°F ensures optimal extraction without scorching delicate flavor notes.
- **Visual Concept**: Gooseneck kettle pouring in slow concentric spirals over a v60.

### Slide 5: 5. Taste the Difference
- **Copy**: Taste the difference yourself. Visit our café or shop single-origin beans online today! Link in bio.
- **Visual Concept**: Finished ceramic mug with latte art on reclaimed wood table.
